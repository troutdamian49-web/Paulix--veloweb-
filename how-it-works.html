Walkthrough

How the demonstration works

Four steps, from browsing a title to seeing a sample confirmation screen. Two steps are genuinely interactive; two are simulated so you can review the experience without a real order or payment.

  1. 01

    Browse the seven titles

    The catalogue lists only the titles confirmed for this review, with search plus subject and series filters.

    Interactive
  2. 02

    Build a demo cart

    Add titles, change quantities or remove items. The header count and demo totals update immediately.

    Interactive
  3. 03

    Review and confirm

    The demo checkout shows the basket, states clearly that payment is not connected, and asks you to acknowledge that before continuing.

    Simulated
  4. 04

    Sample confirmation

    A fictional reference such as DEMO-JIS-XXXXXX is generated in your browser so the confirmation screen can be reviewed.

    Simulated

Being precise

What is real, what is simulated, what is missing

Reviewers should be able to tell instantly which parts of this prototype are working software and which parts are placeholders awaiting school decisions.

Status of each part of the prototype
Part of the prototype Status Detail
Catalogue of seven titles Confirmed for this review These are the titles supplied for the prototype. No additional titles were invented.
Search and filters Interactive Filters by subject and series only. A class filter is intentionally absent.
Demo cart Interactive Add, remove and change quantities. Stored in your browser only.
Demo totals Simulated ₦1,000 per copy placeholder rate used purely to demonstrate added totals.
Real prices To be confirmed by the school. No price is quoted, calculated or implied anywhere in the prototype.
Class lists (SS1, SS2, SS3) To be confirmed by the school. No title is assigned to any class until the school confirms the lists.
Demo checkout Simulated Asks for no personal or payment information at all.
Payment processing Not connected No gateway, no reference, no receipt. Nothing can be paid on this site.
Order references Simulated Fictional samples generated in the browser, prefixed DEMO-JIS-. They are not recognised by the school.
Stock and availability To be confirmed by the school. No stock quantities are shown or implied.
Alerts to the school Not built There is no server, no email and no dashboard — orders never leave your browser.

Before any real deployment

What would replace the placeholders

i

From the school

  • Confirmation that the seven titles are named correctly
  • The correct SS1, SS2 and SS3 book lists
  • Real prices and who collects payment
  • A collection or delivery plan for the books
  • Written approval to use the school’s name and branding
!

Then, if approved

  • A real payment route arranged by the school — never a page asking for card PINs or OTPs
  • A secure server so orders actually reach the school
  • A clear data-handling and privacy policy before any family details are collected
  • A stated process for refunds, corrections and missing books

Until then, this prototype stays a demonstration: not an official school service.
