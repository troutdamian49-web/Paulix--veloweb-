Safety & safeguards

Questions, limits and safeguards

Straight answers about what this prototype does, what it deliberately does not do, and what would have to change before it could become an official school service.

!

Real payment processing is not connected

There is no payment gateway on this site. No payment has been received or verified, and no bank, card, PIN or OTP details are ever requested. Please do not enter such details anywhere here.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official Justice International School service?

No. It is a demo prototype — not an official school service. It was built so the school can review the idea before any approval or real deployment. Nothing here should be treated as a school instruction, price list or order.

Which textbooks are on the list?

Exactly seven titles, and nothing else:

  • New Concept English 1
  • New Concept English 2
  • TONAD Essential Chemistry
  • TONAD Essential Physics
  • New Concept Mathematics 1 (Volume 1)
  • New Concept Mathematics 2 (Volume 2)
  • TONAD Essential Marketing

No extra textbooks, authors or subjects have been added. See the catalogue.

How much do the books cost?

The real price of every title is To be confirmed by the school. No price is quoted or implied.

The demo cart uses a ₦1,000 per copy placeholder rate so that reviewers can see the cart count and totals updating. That figure is not a price, not a quotation and not payable.

Which books does my class need?

That has not been confirmed yet. No title is assigned to SS1, SS2 or SS3 until the school publishes the correct class lists, so this prototype shows no class lists at all. See class lists.

If I place a demo order, do I get the books?

No. A demo order is a simulation that exists only inside your own browser. Nothing is sent to the school, no book is reserved and no one will be expecting a payment from you.

What is the order reference I was shown?

It is a simulated demo reference, generated randomly in your browser (it looks like DEMO-JIS-XXXXXX). It is fictional, it is not recognised by the school, and it must not be presented as proof of purchase or payment.

Will you ask me to pay online?

Not here. This prototype cannot accept payment. In a finished, school-approved service, payment would follow a route arranged by the school itself — never a page asking for your card PIN, bank USSD code or an OTP.

What information does the demo collect about me?

None at all. There are no name, contact, address or payment fields anywhere in this prototype. The only thing stored is the list of titles you placed in the demo cart, kept in your own browser so the page works without a server.

How do I clear the demo data?

Use Clear demo cart on the demo cart page and Remove this simulated order on the simulated order page. Clearing your browser’s site data does the same thing.

What happens next?

The school reviews this prototype, corrects the title names, confirms the class lists and prices, and decided whether the service should go ahead. See how the demo works for the list of outstanding information.

Who made this prototype?

The prototype was produced for the school’s review as a demonstration of a possible online textbook ordering service. It is not affiliated with, published by, or endorsed by any textbook publisher, and the title names are used only to identify the books supplied for this review.

What this demo never asks for

  • Bank account numbers or transfer receipts
  • Card numbers, expiry dates or security codes
  • Card PINs, bank USSD codes or one-time passwords
  • Passwords or ID documents
  • Names, phone numbers, addresses or pupil details

If any website claiming to be a school service asks for these, do not enter them — check with the school office first.

i

Where the demo data lives

The demo cart and simulated order are saved only in this browser (local storage). There is no server, no account and no school database.

Nothing is transmitted anywhere, and nobody else can see it.

!

Spotted a mistake?

If a title is misspelled, a subject is wrong or a detail is missing, please raise it with the school. Corrections will only be made from information the school confirms — this prototype will not guess.

Give feedback
