What you are looking at

A working demonstration, not a live school service.

The prototype exists so the school can judge whether an online textbook ordering service would be useful — and how it should work — before any budget or approval is committed.

Working in this demo

What the prototype actually does

  • Lists the seven textbook titles confirmed for this review — nothing more.
  • Lets you search and filter those titles by subject and series.
  • Adds titles to an interactive demo cart where you can change quantities or remove items.
  • Keeps the cart count and the demo totals up to date as you go.
  • Walks through a demo checkout and produces a simulated order reference you can print.
Not connected

What the prototype deliberately does not do

  • It does not process, request or verify any payment.
  • It does not show real prices, authors, editions, stock figures or class lists.
  • It does not assign any title to SS1, SS2 or SS3.
  • It does not collect bank details, card details, PINs, OTPs or real parent information.
  • It does not send anything to the school — there is no server behind this site.

Class lists

No book is assigned to SS1, SS2 or SS3 yet.

The school has not confirmed which class needs which title, so this prototype shows no class allocations at all. Choosing a class and handing a pupil a fixed book list would be a guess, and a guess is not good enough for a school.

Once the school supplies the correct class lists, each title can be linked to SS1, SS2 or SS3 and the “To be confirmed by the school” labels can be replaced with real information.

Payments & family data

Real payment processing is not connected.

This prototype has no payment gateway and no school database. Please never type bank details, card details, PINs, OTPs or real parent information into a demonstration site.

!

Never asked for — anywhere in this demo

  • Bank account numbers or transfer receipts
  • Debit or credit card numbers, expiry dates or CVVs
  • Card PINs, bank USSD codes or one-time passwords (OTPs)
  • Passwords, ID documents or real pupil or parent details
✓

Where demo data lives

The demo cart and the simulated order are stored only in your own browser, so the prototype still works with no server and no internet connection to a backend.

You can wipe it at any time with Clear demo cart on the cart page or Remove this simulated order on the order page. Clearing your browser data does the same thing.

For the review panel

What the school is being asked to decide.

This demonstration exists to answer five questions before any official approval or real deployment:

  1. Are these the correct textbook titles, and are they correctly named?
  2. Which titles belong to SS1, SS2 and SS3?
  3. What are the real prices, and who collects payment?
  4. How should books be collected or distributed once ordered?
  5. Should the finished service be an official school service at all?

Until those answers arrive, this prototype will keep showing “To be confirmed by the school” wherever a real detail is missing.

Reviewer checklist

  • Check the seven titles are named correctly
  • Try the search, subject and series filters
  • Add, remove and change quantities in the demo cart
  • Walk through the demo checkout
  • Confirm the class lists are still missing
  • Read the safeguards and demo-limits notes
